An entry for the [make.fun bounty "Make the AI judge excited" (ai-excited-01)](https://make.fun/bounty/ai-excited-01).

A giant pink axolotl bursts out of a round glass fishbowl on a wooden desk in a cozy living room — water exploding outward in frozen motion, glass shards flying, the axolotl lunging toward the camera with its feathery external gills spread wide. A small child recoils in awe at the edge of the frame while a cat flees across the desk. Warm lamp light, books and a mug: the most impossible thing, in the most ordinary room.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies the entry image to `/output/entry.png`:

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

The image is `entry.png` — a single photorealistic image, generated for this entry, no text anywhere in it.

## Why this image

The judge weighs excitement first, then surprise, momentum, craft, and how the image reads small. This one is built for that order: one huge central creature that hits at a glance, a twist nobody else on the board used (an axolotl — not a whale — escaping its bowl), frozen mid-burst motion, clean photorealistic craft, and a single shape that stays clear at 300 pixels across.
